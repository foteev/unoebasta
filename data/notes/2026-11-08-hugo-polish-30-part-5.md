# Hugo (HUG) — Polish source checks for 30 more winners

Checked 2026-11-08. This fifth batch covers the next 30 Hugo-winning works in catalog order with a gap in Polish full text or human narration, after excluding the 120 previously checked works. Titles, authors, forms and years are matched to catalog QIDs.

Verified 13 full texts and one additional guest-readable excerpt. Complete texts use external sources with unknown rights. Two downloadable plain-text editions use Windows-1250. Polish retail audio, including the MAG edition of *Hyperion*, remains a lead. No complete guest Polish recording passed review.

- **Q27928758 · The Death of Captain Future (1996)** — Searched Allen Steele and the exact title with Polish translation and audio terms. Found English edition and audiobook records. No Polish guest text verified.
- **Q7747390 · The Lincoln Train (1996)** — Searched Maureen F. McHugh, the original title and Polish title variants. Results included English bibliography and an English collection; no Polish guest reader verified.
- **Q905714 · Think Like a Dinosaur (1996)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b1ef32592a6331e2c8901d4/download). Guest UTF-8 text file identifies James Patrick Kelly and the Polish title. Checked Kamala Shastri’s arrival at Tuulen and her final conversation with the narrator in the shuttle bay. Rights unknown.
- **Q2884527 · Lustrzany taniec (1995)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b144d107a10741e1a6ac10f/download). Guest Windows-1250 text file identifies Lois McMaster Bujold, Mirror Dance and translator Łukasz Praski. Checked the opening, the chapter sequence through chapter 33 and the final scene with Mark and Miles arriving at the Vorkosigan residence. Rights unknown.
- **Q7457497 · Siedem spojrzeń na wąwóz Olduvai (1995)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b140d581388af1306c0e38a/download#page=45). Guest anthology PDF gives Mike Resnick’s original title and Anna Klimasara’s translation credit. The story begins on physical page 45 and concludes on page 99 before Łowy na Snarka. A different web transcription starts partway into the story. Rights unknown.
- **Q7049279 · Drugi wzrok (1995)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b1ef6047a10741e1a6ad067/download). Guest UTF-8 file names Joe Haldeman. Checked Cletus Jefferson’s opening question, the final contrast between two forms of blindness, and the biographical note identifying the original Hugo-winning story. The copy’s translator line is incomplete. Rights unknown.
- **Q16955002 · The Martian Child (1995)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b1ef6047a10741e1a6ad065/download). Guest UTF-8 file identifies David Gerrold. Checked the adoption-office opening about Dennis and the final scene before the magazine note, which identifies this as the Hugo-winning short work. Rights unknown.
- **Q3294970 · Zielony Mars (1994)** — Confirmed Polish edition and the existing Vesper excerpt via naEKRANIE. The LibCat results that surfaced were other Mars trilogy volumes, including Blue Mars. No new Polish full text verified.
- **Q27980709 · Down in the Bottomlands (1994)** — Searched Harry Turtledove, exact title, Polish variants and magazine terms. The surfaced audiobook and author bibliography were English.
- **Q27976015 · Śmierć na Nilu (1994)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b1ef1cb9a4fad1e277ab449/download). Guest UTF-8 file identifies Connie Willis. Checked Zoe reading aboard the plane to Egypt, the chapter structure and the final stairway scene mentioning Osiris and Poirot. This is Willis’s short story. Rights unknown.
- **Q5547948 · Georgia on My Mind (1994)** — Searched Charles Sheffield, exact title and Polish-title variants. Surfaced the English collection and bibliographic records. No Polish guest source verified.
- **Q2603313 · Księga Sądu Ostatecznego (1993)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5ab3c54d61fc20799523218a/download). Guest 446-page PDF identifies Connie Willis, Doomsday Book and translator Arkadiusz Nakoniecznik. Checked the opening, chapter 36 and the closing scene with Kivrin and Dunworthy before the biographical material and notes. Rights unknown.
- **Q58221164 · Ogień nad otchłanią (1993)** — [Booktown / eLibri · fragment](https://www.elibri.com.pl/excerpt/357344/b4ff3049940d40dd88f8ce78311a0181/ogien-nad-otchlania-fragment.epub). Guest EPUB download is linked from Booktown. Its editorial page identifies Vernor Vinge’s A Fire Upon the Deep. XHTML contains the prologue and actual Polish narrative through part of chapter seven, followed by a purchase notice. This is an additional format to the existing Legimi sample.
- **Q21096914 · Ballada o Billu Pąklarzu (1993)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b1ef1c97a10741e1a6acf6d/download). Guest UTF-8 text file identifies Lucius Shepard, Barnacle Bill the Spacer and translator Michał Jakuszewski. Checked the narrator’s opening and the concluding passage before the repeated translation credit. Rights unknown.
- **Q5416574 · Nawet królowa (1993)** — [Zanotowane · full](https://zanotowane.pl/237/6911/#:~:text=NAWET%20KR%C3%93LOWA%20Prze%C5%82o%C5%BCy%C5%82a%20Dorota%20Malinowska). Checked Miles’s opening assignment, chapter 17 and his final exchange with the head of Imperial Security. Rights unknown.
- **Q7739362 · The Hemingway Hoax (1991)** — Searched Joe Haldeman, exact title and Polish variants. The surfaced Spanish anthology and Russian FantLab record did not establish Polish guest access.
- **Q4287396 · Niedźwiedzie odkrywają ogień (1991)** — Polish print lead *Niedźwiedzie odkrywają ogień*, *Isaac Asimov’s Science Fiction* 01/1991. No guest narrative or complete Polish audio verified.
- **Q27978004 · The Manamouki (1991)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b140d581388af1306c0e38a/download#page=364). Guest Polish collection PDF, physical pages 364–398.
