## 1. Summary of Verified Additions

This batch adds six verified unabridged Spanish texts for classic Hugo, Nebula, Locus, and World Fantasy Award winners:

1. **Q4540312** — *"Repent, Harlequin!" Said the Ticktockman* (*«¡Arrepiéntete, Arlequín!» dijo el señor Tic-Tac*) by Harlan Ellison (Hugo Award 1966, Nebula Award 1966 Best Short Story). Complete Spanish translation by Paula Tizzano, Márgara Averbach, and María Cristina Pinto; 4,700 words.
2. **Q16386319** — *Inconstant Moon* (*Luna inconstante*) by Larry Niven (Hugo Award 1972 Best Short Story). Complete Spanish translation by Juan Pablo Guevara; 9,160 words.
3. **Q2045767** — *The Man in the Black Suit* (*El hombre del traje negro*) by Stephen King (World Fantasy Award 1995, O. Henry Award 1996). Complete Spanish translation by Bettina Blanch Tyroller; 9,886 words.
4. **Q7992621** — *When It Changed* (*Cuando las cosas cambiaron*) by Joanna Russ (Nebula Award 1973 Best Short Story). Complete Spanish translation by Márgara Auerbach, María Cristina Pinto, and Paula Tizzano; 3,396 words.
5. **Q6148016** — *All Dracula's Children* (*Todos los hijos de Drácula*) by Dan Simmons (Locus Award 1992 Best Novelette). Complete Spanish translation by Francisco Rodríguez de Lecea; 10,314 words.
6. **Q20737828** — *Bloodchild* (*Hija de sangre*) by Octavia E. Butler (Hugo Award 1985, Nebula Award 1984, Locus Award 1985 Best Novelette). Complete unabridged Spanish translation; 7,731 words.
