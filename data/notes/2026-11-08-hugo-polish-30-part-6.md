# Hugo Polish source search · batch 6

Checked 30 Hugo winners against the current catalog, prioritizing Polish text, excerpts and human narration. This batch contains four full texts and six excerpts. No free Polish audio was verified. PDF-X entries use readable HTML previews. When a preview ends early or the full download cannot be verified, it is treated as an excerpt. External hosting rights remain unverified.

- **Q629325 · Kirinyaga (1989)** — Verified full Polish short story in [PDF-X](https://pdf-x.pl/doc/Resnick-Mike-Kirinyaga/5b1ef326a32a9d1e1f7224f3). The [Prószyński book](https://www.proszynski.pl/product/kirinyaga) is the later collection; its listing does not establish access to the award-winning story.
- **Q7432921 · Schrödinger's Kitten (1989)** — [Don Wollheim proponuje 1989](https://www.encyklopediafantastyki.pl/index.php?title=Don_Wollheim_proponuje_1989) confirms Jacek Manicki’s translation, *Kociątko Schrödingera*. No guest Polish text or human narration verified.
- **Q2502176 · The Uplift War (1988)** — [RuLit](https://www.rulit.me/books/wojna-wspomaganych-the-uplift-war-pl-read-266231-2.html) opens the Polish narrative. Page 1 is a glossary and the ending was not verified. Submitted as an excerpt.
- **Q5422656 · Eye for Eye (1988)** — [Booxy](https://www.booxy.pl/ksiazka-181083-oko-za-oko-eye-for-eye) confirms *Oko za oko* as a Polish publication lead. The [Storytel listing](https://www.storytel.com/pl/books/eye-for-eye-15071870) is English. No guest Polish text or narration verified.
- **Q7997801 · Why I Left Harry's All-Night Hamburgers (1988)** — [Polska Bibliografia Literacka](https://pbl.ibl.poznan.pl/dostep/index.php?f=zapisy&p_zrodlo=2285&s=d_biezacy) confirms *No i rzuciłem robotę u Harry’ego*. No open Polish text or human narration verified.
- **Q4985727 · Buffalo Gals, Won't You Come Out Tonight (1988)** — [Dziewczyny Buffalo i inne zwierzęce obecności](https://encyklopediafantastyki.pl/index.php?title=Dziewczyny_Buffalo_i_inne_zwierz%C4%99ce_obecno%C5%9Bci) is a Polish collection lead. No guest copy of the assigned story or human narration verified.
- **Q2291295 · Speaker for the Dead (1987)** — [PDF-X](https://pdf-x.pl/doc/Card-Orson-Scott-Mowca-umarlych/5b1ee7aea32a9d1e1f722359) opens the Polish preview. The download and its ending were not verified. [Audioteka](https://audioteka.com/pl/audiobook/mowca-umarlych1/) is a commercial narration lead.
- **Q3190243 · Gilgamesh in the Outback (1987)** — [Onet Kultura](https://kultura.onet.pl/fragmenty-ksiazek/gilgamesz-na-pustkowiu-fragment-ksiazki-pozeglowac-do-bizancjum/bl6rtnh) opens the exact Polish excerpt. The anthology preview on PDF-X did not establish the ending. No free Polish narration verified.
- **Q27976003 · Tangents (1987)** — Verified the full Polish story in [Zanotowane](https://zanotowane.pl/239/3447/), translated by Jacek Manicki. No free Polish narration verified.
- **Q7169194 · Permafrost (1987)** — [PDF-X](https://pdf-x.pl/doc/Zelazny-Roger-Wieczna-marzloc/5b1ef3297a10741e1a6ad035) opens the Polish story but its HTML preview cuts off mid-word. Submitted as an excerpt. The download and free Polish audio were not verified.
