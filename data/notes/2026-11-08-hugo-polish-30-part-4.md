# Hugo (HUG) — Polish source checks for 30 more winners

Checked 2026-11-08. This fourth batch covers the next 30 Hugo-winning works in catalog order with a gap in Polish full text or human narration, after excluding the 90 works already checked. Titles, authors, forms and years are matched to catalog QIDs.

Verified eight full texts and three guest-readable excerpts. The full texts are external sources with unknown rights. The two LibCat readers were intermittently rate-limited and their final pages loaded on sequential retries. Paid recordings for *Koralina*, *Amerykańscy bogowie*, *Harry Potter i Czara Ognia* and *Wieczny pokój* remain leads. No complete open Polish recording passed review.

- **Q6518178 · Legions in Time (2004)** — Published as *Legiony temporalne* in *Rakietowe szlaki* 5 and *Legiony czasu* in the MAG Swanwick omnibus. The guest omnibus EPUB reached only *Próżniowe kwiaty*. No guest narrative for the assigned story verified.
- **Q19346391 · Hominidzi (2003)** — Polish edition *Hominidzi* confirmed in PBL and library records. Exact title and author searches yielded print records. The author-site excerpts already in the catalog are English.
- **Q475125 · Koralina (2003)** — [Ebookpoint · fragment](https://ebookpoint.pl/pobierz-fragment/koralina-neil-gaiman,e_1988/epub). Guest EPUB was downloaded and its XHTML inspected. Editorial page identifies Neil Gaiman and the original title. It contains about 1,600 words of the opening chapter, then a full-edition notice. The remaining chapter headings appear only in the contents.
- **Q5432331 · Falling Onto Mars (2003)** — Searched Geoffrey A. Landis, the exact original title and Polish-title variants. The Polish anthology search returned *Wyprawa do czarnej dziury*, a different Landis story, and a Czech listing for the assigned work.
- **Q7542032 · Slow Life (2003)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b1ed1b57a10741e1a6acc2d/download#page=136). Guest anthology PDF contains Michael Swanwick’s Slow Life on physical pages 136–159. Checked the opening, the concluding exchange, Jolanta Pers’s translation credit, and the author note on page 160. Rights unknown.
- **Q430514 · Amerykańscy bogowie (2002)** — [ЛибКат · full](https://libcat.ru/knigi/fantastika-i-fjentezi/fentezi/227308-neil-gaiman-ameryka-scy-bogowie.html#text). Guest Polish reader identifies Neil Gaiman and opens on Shadow’s imprisonment. Checked intermediate narrative and pages 130–133, including the Iceland ending on page 132, author acknowledgments, and translator’s note. Late pages returned 429 during parallel access and opened on sequential retry. Rights unknown.
- **Q19588505 · Fast Times at Fairmont High (2002)** — Searched Vernor Vinge and the original title, including the Fairmont setting, for a Polish edition or recording. The surfaced Apple Books recording is English.
- **Q7730518 · Hau-hau, powiedział pies (2002)** — Bibliographic leads: *Hau-hau, powiedział pies* in *Nowa Fantastyka* 03/2003 and *Pies powiedział „hau”* in the MAG Swanwick omnibus. Guest omnibus EPUB contains only the beginning of a different work.
- **Q5706622 · Piekło to nieobecność Boga (2002)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5aca862f3d0b1b0deeb8a3cd/download#page=201). Guest Polish anthology PDF. Ted Chiang and both titles match. Neil Fisk’s story runs from physical page 201 to page 230, where the closing paragraph is followed by Michał Jakuszewski’s translation credit. Another story starts on page 231. Rights unknown.
- **Q46751 · Harry Potter i Czara Ognia (2001)** — [Blogspot · fragment](https://hp-i-czara-ognia.blogspot.com/2017/01/rozdzia-pierwszy-dom-riddleow.html). Guest blog contains roughly 4,300 words of chapter one, Dom Riddle’ów. The archive lists chapters 1–14 and chapter 14 also contains Polish narrative. No later chapters or complete novel were verified. Rights unknown.
- **Q27946225 · The Ultimate Earth (2001)** — Searched Jack Williamson and the exact original title with Polish translation and audio terms. Surfaced English book and Apple Books records. No guest Polish text verified.
- **Q27969102 · Różne rodzaje ciemności (2001)** — Polish print lead *Różne rodzaje ciemności*, *Nowa Fantastyka* 05 (296)/2007. Title and issue records match David Langford. No guest story reader verified.
- **Q27969107 · Millennium Babies (2001)** — Searched the exact title and Kristine Kathryn Rusch with Polish translation, magazine and audio terms. The English Kobo edition and Polish-language bibliography do not establish Polish guest access.
- **Q2292400 · Otchłań w niebie (2000)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5ac944b65bc28c0e2e5b6a80/download). Guest Polish PDF, 600 physical pages. Vernor Vinge and title on cover. Inspected the prologue and middle narrative and the epilogue beginning on page 589, including the final conversation on page 600. Rights unknown.
- **Q21190041 · Wichry w Marble Arch (2000)** — Bibliographic leads: *Wichry Marmurowego Łuku* in *SFinks* 06/2002 and *Wichry w Marble Arch* in *Niebieski księżyc*. Searches by both titles and collection did not verify open narrative.
- **Q7431214 · Scherzo z tyranozaurem (2000)** — Listed in *Nowa Fantastyka* 09 (252)/2003 and the MAG Swanwick omnibus. The guest omnibus EPUB ends in *Próżniowe kwiaty*, before the target.
- **Q4546842 · 10¹⁶ to 1 (2000)** — Searched title variants using the superscript, decimal notation and Polish numerals with James Patrick Kelly. English Clarkesworld and bibliography results surfaced. The *Kroki w nieznane 2007* search did not confirm this target in the anthology.
- **Q2719299 · Nie licząc psa (1999)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5ab3c54d1b52ec798f72e8e2/download). Guest Polish PDF, 364 pages. Edition identifies Connie Willis, original title and Danuta Górska. Checked chapter 1, the final conversation of chapter 28 on page 361, and the bibliographic note that follows. Rights unknown.
- **Q7076198 · Oceanic (1999)** — Searched Greg Egan, the exact title and Polish title variants. The Polish-facing Libristo result is an English e-book. No matching guest Polish text or audio verified.
- **Q7772469 · Prawdziwy puls maszyny (1999)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/6609d8e35ccf8c00176d8a01/download#page=126). Guest Polish anthology PDF. Checked Michael Swanwick’s title and Martha’s opening on physical page 126, her final jump and translator’s note on page 156, and the following author on page 157. The HTML copy at Zanotowane is another rendition of the same anthology. Rights unknown.
- **Q7678446 · Taklamakan (1999)** — Polish print lead: *Takla Makan*, *Fenix* 02 (91)/2000. Opened LibCat’s actual narrative, which is English despite the multilingual search result. No Polish reader or audio verified.
- **Q9183865 · Wieczny pokój (1998)** — [Ebookpoint · fragment](https://ebookpoint.pl/pobierz-fragment/wieczny-pokoj-wieczna-wojna-tom-2-joe-haldeman,e_4l50/epub). Guest EPUB editorial page identifies Joe Haldeman’s Forever Peace. Chapter 1 contains about 2,700 words of Polish narrative and ends in a purchase notice. The Woblink sample has the same chapter and cutoff. Paid audio remains a lead.
- **Q27928768 · "…Where Angels Fear to Tread" (1998)** — Searched original title and Allen Steele with Polish translation and narration terms. The surfaced Apple Books and Audible recordings are English.
- **Q7711914 · 43 dynastie antaryjskie (1998)** — [PDF-X.PL · full](https://pdf-x.pl/api/document/5b140d581388af1306c0e38a/download#page=5). Guest Polish collection PDF, pages 5–21. Checked the original title and Anna Klimasara credit, the ending with Perganian and the diamond, and the different story that begins on page 22. Rights unknown.
- **Q27978104 · We Will Drink a Fish Together… (1998)** — Searched the original title and Bill Johnson using Polish bibliography and translation terms. SFWA and SFADB establish identity; a Czech record did not establish a Polish edition.
- **Q3294967 · Błękitny Mars (1997)** — [ЛибКат · full](https://libcat.ru/knigi/fantastika-i-fjentezi/227247-kim-robinson-b-kitny-mars.html#text). Guest Polish reader identifies Kim Stanley Robinson. Checked title and dedication, early and intermediate narrative, Ann’s closing beach scene on page 213, and acknowledgments on page 214. Late pages returned 429 during parallel access and opened on sequential retry. Rights unknown.
- **Q27945849 · Blood of the Dragon (1997)** — Bibliography identifies the novella as the Daenerys chapters of *Gra o tron*. *Ścieżka smoka* and *Ogień i krew* are different works. No guest Polish source with the award novella’s exact chapter scope verified.
- **Q27976025 · Dusza dobiera sobie towarzystwo: Inwazja i odraza: chronologiczna reinterpretacja dwóch utworów Emily Dickinson: Perspektywa Wellsowska (1997)** — Polish print lead in *Echa wojny światów*, translated by Katarzyna Rzepka and Jacek Matwiejczyk. Searched title and anthology; no guest narrative verified.
- **Q4903762 · Bicycle Repairman (1997)** — Bibliographic lead *Naprawiacz rowerów* in *To, co najlepsze w SF 2* and *Najlepsze opowiadania science fiction roku 1996*. No guest Polish reader or complete recording verified.
- **Q543562 · Diamentowy wiek (1996)** — Publisher and bibliography identify *Diamentowy wiek*, also published as *Epoka diamentu*. Neither retailer descriptions nor the publisher product page established a guest Polish reader.
