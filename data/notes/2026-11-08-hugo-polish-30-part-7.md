# Hugo Polish source search, batch 7

Checked 2026-11-08. This batch covers the next 30 Hugo-winning works in catalog order with a Polish full-text or human-narration gap. It excludes the 180 works checked in batches 1–6, including the open sixth submission. Scope is Polish text, excerpts and human narration.

The submission contains 16 complete texts, three separate excerpts and one full audio link across 16 works. Full texts were checked at the opening and conclusion. External hosting permission is unverified. Word counts are estimates. The audio link adds a playable YouTube option for a work already catalogued on Apple Podcasts. Two other YouTube uploads were promotional excerpts. The PDF-X entries marked full link to the PDF opened from the public download control. The Scribd magazine transcriptions may interleave printed columns.
