# Hugo (HUG) — Polish source checks for 30 more winners

Checked 2026-11-08. The third batch covers the next 30 Hugo-winning works in catalog order with a gap in Polish full text or human narration, after excluding the 60 works already checked. Titles, authors, forms and years are matched to catalog QIDs.

Verified nine full texts and three guest-readable excerpts. Complete texts on external hosts have unknown permission and retain their evidence and check date. The Polish city-portal audio sample and paid audio listings remain leads. No complete Polish guest recording passed review. Gaps below reflect the checked queries and editions.
