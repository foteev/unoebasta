# Hugo (HUG) — Polish source checks for 30 more winners

Checked 2026-11-07. This is the next 30 works in Hugo winner catalog order after the previous batch, with a gap in Polish full text or human-narrated audio. Titles and authors were matched to catalog QIDs. Results concern guest access at the time of checking.

Four complete Polish texts and four excerpts passed review. The full texts comprise a magazine publication and three direct guest PDF downloads with unknown rights, recorded as external. One excerpt provides an alternative to an existing retailer link. No complete, guest-playable Polish audio passed review. Retail audio listings and sample buttons were treated as leads.

- **Q69991268 · A Witch's Guide to Escape: A Practical Compendium of Portal Fantasies (2019)** — Polish print publication: *Przewodnik Wiedźmy po Ucieczkach: Praktyczne Kompendium Fantazji o Portalach*, [Nowa Fantastyka 8/2020](https://www.fantastyka.pl/czasopisma), p. 40, translated by Jerzy Rzymowski. No guest narrative or complete narration verified.
- **Q65057492 · If at First You Don't Succeed, Try, Try Again (2019)** — Exact original title and Zen Cho searched with Polish translation, magazine and audio terms. No matching guest source verified.
- **Q39058554 · Kamienne niebo (2018)** — The catalog already has a Virtualo excerpt. [Audioteka](https://audioteka.com/pl/audiobook/kamienne-niebo/) offers a sample and sells the complete Polish recording.
- **Q53828903 · Wszystkie wskaźniki czerwone (2018)** — [Wszystkie wskaźniki czerwone · Legimi](https://www.legimi.pl/ebook-pamietniki-mordbota-wszystkie-wskazniki-czerwone-sztuczny-stan-martha-wells,b1053816.html#sample_section). Guest HTML sample contains about 5,250 words from Martha Wells’s opening Murderbot novella, translated by Marek Pawelec. The sample explicitly ends with a purchase prompt. It does not include the second novella named in the listing.
- **Q56215021 · Welcome to your Authentic Indian Experience™ (2018)** — Exact original title and Rebecca Roanhorse searched. No matching guest Polish text or audio verified.

Jemisin’s novel contains the Polish opening narrative addressed to Essun. It is a standalone readable sample on Gavran, distinct from the existing retailer listing.
- **Q30004969 · Każde serce to wrota (2017)** — The catalog already has a Legimi excerpt. Polish audio listings offer a sample or paid access to the complete recording.
